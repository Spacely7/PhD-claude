Featured research
Master's thesis

Development and analysis of a real-time port activity monitoring dashboard in Ghana

Research methodDesign Science Research
Research domainPort operations & analytics
Core methodsML · Visual analytics · Decision support

The problem

Ports generate substantial volumes of operational and trade data, but transforming these data into timely and actionable intelligence can be challenging.

The research investigated how data analytics, machine learning, and interactive visualisation could be integrated into a decision-support artefact for port activity monitoring in Ghana.

Research aim

To develop and analyse a real-time port activity monitoring dashboard that integrates data analytics, machine learning, and interactive visualisation to support operational monitoring and decision-making in Ghana.

Data

The study used Ghana Customs Bills of Entry data containing operational, financial, temporal, geographical, cargo, and risk-related attributes.

Customs data→ Data preparation→ Exploratory analysis→ Predictive analytics→ Decision support

Methodology

The research adopted a Design Science Research approach to guide the development and evaluation of the decision-support artefact.

Machine learning

The study compared multiple machine-learning approaches for the classification task investigated in the research.

Logistic regressionDecision treeRandom forest Gradient boostingSVMKNN XGBoostLightGBM

Model evaluation considered metrics including accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, while the analysis also considered the implications of class imbalance.

Looking beyond prediction

Predictive performance alone does not explain why a model produces a particular result. The research therefore incorporated feature-importance analysis and SHAP-based interpretation to investigate factors influencing model predictions.

Prediction → explanation → decision. This perspective has contributed to my growing interest in explainable and human-centred intelligent decision systems.

Interactive decision support

The research developed a Power BI-based proof-of-concept dashboard designed to consolidate analytical information into an interactive monitoring environment.

Operational overview

Consolidated analytical indicators for exploring port activity.

Geographic analysis

Exploration of geographical patterns within the data.

Commodity analysis

Examination of commodity and trade patterns.

Analytical exploration of risk-related information.

Contribution

Applied contribution

Demonstrated an approach for integrating data analytics and machine learning within a Ghanaian port operations context.

Artefact contribution

Developed a proof-of-concept interactive decision-support dashboard.

Analytical contribution

Compared predictive approaches while considering evaluation and class imbalance.

Future foundation

Established directions for further work involving explainability, richer data, and human-centred decision support.

Limitations & learning

The research was developed as a proof-of-concept and was constrained by the available dataset and scope of the study. The work also highlighted challenges involving class imbalance, data availability, additional data integration, and the need to evaluate predictive systems within realistic decision-making conditions.

A predictive system should be evaluated not only by how accurately it predicts, but also by how, when, and why its predictions can responsibly support decisions.
